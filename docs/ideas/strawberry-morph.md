# Strawberry Morph

**Status:** Idea
**Plugins:** MorphSVGPlugin

## The idea

The site is called Cosmic Strawberry and has zero strawberries on it. The COSMIC
letters are already pure geometry — half-circles, a circle, triangles, a rect.
Morph them: the sequence plays, holds, then all six letterforms flow into a
single strawberry silhouette (body, leaves, seeds picked out by the cyan stars),
hold, and flow back.

The brand joke lands visually instead of only in the text.

## How it works

MorphSVG between the existing letter paths and a strawberry path set. Needs a
strawberry drawn in the same visual language as the letters — flat geometric
shapes in the brand palette, not an illustrated berry.

## Open questions

- Six shapes into one silhouette means some paths merge or shrink to nothing.
- Does the strawberry hold as a resting state, or is it a passing beat?
